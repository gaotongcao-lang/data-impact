Climate change, community and mental health across the Pacific.

An interactive platform exploring where climate change exposure, community characteristics and mental-health vulnerability overlap. Interactive maps and Sankey flow diagrams bring together disaster exposure, temperature and precipitation anomalies, rural population share and estimated suicide mortality rates, so users can explore patterns across countries and years.

An exploratory view of overlapping indicators, not an estimate of causal effects.
