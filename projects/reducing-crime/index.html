1st Place, Winter Data Analysis Challenge 2026

I collaborated on a project for the Data Science Winter Analysis competition, exploring whether Australia was doing well. We approached this question through the lens of safety and security. Using Google Trends and Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) data, we built a multilinear regression model with predictors including physical assault, robbery, and vehicle theft. We found a counterintuitive pattern: increases in these crime types were associated with increased perceived safety. This unexpected relationship offers a starting point for further investigation into how crime and perceptions of safety connect in Australia.
