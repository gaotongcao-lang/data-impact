how we work

Explore. Model. Decide. Impact.

Every piece of work follows the same path, from an open question to a result someone can act on.

  1. 01

    Explore

    Understand the question, the people it affects and the data available. Look before modelling.

  2. 02

    Model

    Choose statistical and machine learning methods that fit the problem, and be honest about uncertainty.

  3. 03

    Decide

    Turn results into clear visuals and plain-language findings that support a real decision.

  4. 04

    Impact

    Share the story behind the work so it reaches the people who can use it.
